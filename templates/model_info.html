Acoustic Markers & Biomechanical Principles

1. Jitter (Pitch Period Micro-Perturbation)

Human vocal fold vibration is inherently non-periodic due to biomechanical fluctuations, turbulent mucosal wave dynamics, and neuromuscular noise. Synthetic speech engines often exhibit either unnatural period rigidity or quantized synthetic jitter.

Jitter(local) = [ 1 / (N - 1) * Σ |T[i] - T[i+1]| ] / [ 1 / N * Σ T[i] ]

2. Shimmer (Cycle-to-Cycle Amplitude Variation)

Measures the variability in peak amplitude between adjacent glottal cycles. Reflects glottal closure resistance and transglottal airflow variations.

Shimmer(local) = [ 1 / (N - 1) * Σ |A[i] - A[i+1]| ] / [ 1 / N * Σ A[i] ]

3. Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio (HNR)

Quantifies the proportion of harmonic energy produced by regular vocal fold vibration versus aperiodic turbulent noise generated during aspiration. Computed via harmonic-percussive source separation:

HNR (dB) = 10 * log10( P_harmonic / P_noise )

4. Spectral Contrast & Octave Sub-Bands

Evaluates the energy difference between spectral peaks (harmonics) and valleys across 7 logarithmic frequency bands. Synthetic vocoders often show smoothed spectral valleys or unnatural contrast discontinuities.

5. Prosody & Temporal Rhythms

Quantifies vocal dynamics including pitch coefficient of variation (F0 std / F0 mean), voiced frame ratios, and natural breathing pause ratios.

Operational Boundaries & Forensic Limitations

• Codec & Compression Degradation: Telephony networks (GSM/AMR codecs) and VoIP platforms (e.g. WhatsApp, Zoom) heavily downsample and compress speech, discarding high-frequency phase and introducing synthetic-like compression artifacts.

• Environmental Reverberation: Hard-walled rooms and cheap laptop microphones distort spectral contrast and lower measured HNR independently of vocal authenticity.

• Next-Generation Diffusion Vocoders: Modern diffusion models (such as Audiobox and Voicebox) produce increasingly organic glottal pulses, requiring ongoing dataset retraining with novel generative architectures.

• Forensic Use Standard: Voice Sentinel outputs probabilistic model confidence rather than infallible proof. Forensic investigators must cross-validate acoustic analysis with metadata, contextual verification, and network provenance.
