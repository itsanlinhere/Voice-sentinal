Training Architecture & Design Principles

• No Hard-Coded Rules: Predictions do not rely on static thresholds (such as "if jitter > X, it's AI"). The Random Forest constructs non-linear decision trees evaluating multivariate interactions between pitch micro-perturbation, spectral contrast, and formant envelope stability.

• Class Balancing: The model uses class_weight='balanced' to penalize mistakes proportionally when human and synthetic classes differ in size.

• Artifact Persistence: Trained models and scalers are saved as serialized binaries in models/voice_detector.pkl and models/scaler.pkl alongside feature name registries for deterministic inference.
